# Health Insurance

## Basic Health Plan

The Basic Health Plan provides health insurance coverage of ₹5 lakh.

Features:

- Annual premium of ₹7,500
- Cashless hospitalization
- Free annual health check-up
- Pre-existing disease waiting period of 3 years

This plan is suitable for individuals seeking affordable health coverage.

---

## Premium Health Plan

The Premium Health Plan provides health insurance coverage of ₹10 lakh.

Features:

- Annual premium of ₹12,000
- ICU coverage included
- Ambulance charges covered up to ₹5,000
- Cashless hospitalization
- Annual health check-up

This plan offers higher protection and additional benefits compared to the Basic Health Plan.

---

## Family Floater Plan

The Family Floater Plan provides health insurance coverage of ₹15 lakh.

Features:

- Covers two adults and two children
- Annual premium of ₹16,500
- Cashless hospitalization

This plan is designed for families seeking a single policy.

# Life Insurance

## Secure Life Plan

The Secure Life Plan provides life insurance coverage of ₹50 lakh.

Features:

- Entry age 18 to 55 years
- Policy term 10 to 30 years
- Monthly or yearly premium payment options

---

## Lifetime Protection Plan

The Lifetime Protection Plan provides life insurance coverage of ₹1 crore.

Features:

- Entry age 18 to 60 years
- Coverage continues until age 75
- Accidental death benefit included

# Claim Process

To submit a claim:

1. Inform Darwix Insurance within 24 hours.
2. Share the policy number.
3. Upload required documents.
4. Complete verification.
5. Receive payment after approval.

Typical claim processing time is 2 to 5 working days.

---

- Policy number
- Aadhaar Card
