# Philippines Native-Language Voice Bot

## Market

Life Insurance

## Supported Speech

- English
- Filipino
- Taglish

## Code-Switching

The bot should continue naturally when customers mix English and Filipino.

Examples:

- "Magkano ang premium?"
- "Pwede bang monthly ang payment?"

## Common Terms

- premium
- policy
- beneficiary
- coverage
- rider
- lapse

## Customer Style

- Respectful
- Natural
